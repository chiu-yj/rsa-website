01 / Mission

From complex inputs
to clear choices.

Residential Site Analyzer began as an independent architectural research and development project exploring how people interpret the environment around residential design.

The long-term ambition is a usable and scientifically responsible product platform that serves researchers, designers and people making early-stage site decisions. Its identity is built around spatial evidence, transparency and better human judgment—not claims of artificial certainty.

A statement of intent
“Better environmental decisions begin when analysis becomes understandable, checkable and useful.”

02 / Project lead

Independent work.
Long-term thinking.

Yu-Jyun Chiu (CHIU YU JYUN)

Independent developer & graduate researcher

RSA is developed by Yu-Jyun Chiu in connection with graduate architectural studies at the National University of Kaohsiung, Taiwan. The research focus includes site environmental analysis, evidence communication and the relationship between technical interpretation and design decisions.

This page describes a future-facing company concept—not a claim that a registered company, staffed team, investor group or commercial operation already exists.

04 / Project status & transparency

An ambitious vision.
An honest status.

RSA is currently an independent research prototype.It has a public website, a dedicated project contact email and a software development history. It is not incorporated as a company, does not claim paying clients, investors, institutional sponsorship, or a public commercial software release. Proposed RSA Studio and RSA Intelligence offerings are conceptual. The academic association is personal research context, not authorization to represent National University of Kaohsiung.
Is Residential Site Analyzer a registered company?

No. This website presents a professional brand and future architectural technology company vision, but RSA has not been incorporated.

Are there established customers or team members?

No customers, staff members or partners are represented as confirmed unless they are publicly documented. The project is independently led by Yu-Jyun Chiu.

Are Core, Studio and Intelligence all available?

RSA Core names the existing working research foundation. Studio and Intelligence are proposed future product areas; neither is presently sold as a finished product.

Is RSA an official university product?

No. Graduate research context at the National University of Kaohsiung does not imply university ownership, endorsement or permission to act on its behalf.

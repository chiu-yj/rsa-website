Futian · Seven Residences

福田案 · 七戶透天住宅

Turning residential planning into built experience.

01Behind the work

Design approach

Leading the developer-side process from planning to handover: coordinating plans, elevations and details with the design team, construction interfaces and quality, buyer change requests, procurement and sales support.

Connection to research

Closing the loop between design, site and sales showed how often site conditions surface too late—the gap RSA now studies.

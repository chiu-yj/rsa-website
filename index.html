01 / The challenge

Simulation is powerful.
Understanding it is harder.

Environmental models often produce specialized outputs. RSA explores an additional question: can those outputs be translated into evidence people can understand, compare, and use—without obscuring assumptions or uncertainty?

02 / Research pillars

Three environmental lenses.
One decision framework.

01 / SOLAR

Solar exposure
& shading

Explore how buildings and surrounding context influence daylight exposure and direct-shading patterns across time.

02 / WIND

Wind
environment

Investigate exploratory wind-related context and visualize directional relationships, with clear limits on model fidelity.

03 / THERMAL

Outdoor
thermal comfort

Investigate UTCI-oriented interpretation to communicate outdoor thermal stress and support evidence-based discussion.

03 / Evidence-to-decision workflow

From raw conditions
to explainable insight.

RSA separates environmental computation from interpretation, aiming to preserve provenance and support responsible use.

01Site context

Represent a residential site, surrounding buildings, available spatial data, and modeling assumptions.

02Environmental assessment

Explore solar, wind-related, and thermal-comfort evidence at relevant temporal and spatial scales.

03Evidence translation

Present findings with readable visualization, uncertainty, and traceable analytical context.

04Design exploration

Compare options and study whether clearer evidence improves understanding and early decisions.

04 / Development transparency

A research prototype.
Not a finished product.

RSA is being developed and evaluated iteratively. Features, modeling assumptions, and validation results are not all at the same level of maturity.

Current research & development

Active prototype work

  • Python / Streamlit-based research interface
  • Solar and shading analysis workflows
  • Exploratory wind and UTCI-oriented analysis
  • Building context, floor-level and temporal views
  • Quality assurance, scientific validation, and explainability studies
Planned / Under investigation

AI-assisted interpretation

  • Structured explanations grounded in model outputs
  • Traceable assessment summaries and reports
  • Comparison of design alternatives and trade-offs
  • User studies measuring comprehension and decision support
  • Potential Claude API integration, subject to testing and access
Research integrity note: RSA is intended as an early-stage support tool. It does not replace validated specialist workflows such as detailed Radiance, CFD, or building-energy simulation. No illustrative graphic on this page should be interpreted as measured performance or completed scientific validation.

Data & validation strategy

Research records describe OpenStreetMap, Taiwan National Land Surveying and Mapping Center (NLSC) / official 3D information, climate sources including Taiwan CWA and ERA5, and manual checks where appropriate.

Source provenance, building-height uncertainty, controlled comparison conditions and reproducible validation are ongoing research priorities. Mentioned sources are not a guarantee that every data connector is currently active.

06 / Planned Claude API integration

AI that explains evidence.
Not invents it.

Claude is being evaluated as a future interpretation layer—separate from environmental solvers and grounded in structured, provenance-aware data.

Evidence-grounded explanations

Convert validated model outputs, comparison parameters, limitations and uncertainties into clear language for architects and non-experts.

Decision & report assistance

Explore alternatives, formulate questions and draft traceable assessment reports; AI-generated advice would be labeled and subject to human review.

Status: proposed integration only. No Claude API deployment or measured AI-assisted user-study outcome is claimed.

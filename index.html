01 / Product value

One site. More clarity.
Better-informed design.

RSA is being built for the difficult decisions that come before detailed design: where a building sits, what its surroundings change, and how environmental evidence can be explained without hiding uncertainty.

01 — CONTEXT

See the whole site.

Bring target parcels, neighboring massing and scenario assumptions together rather than interpreting disconnected files.

02 — EVIDENCE

Understand the differences.

Explore solar, directional wind and UTCI-oriented outputs through time- and building-aware representations.

03 — DECISION

Make results usable.

Translate technical findings into transparent comparisons and research-ready communication, with limitations clearly stated.

01 / Why RSA

Simulation is powerful.
Understanding it is harder.

RSA investigates a critical gap between specialist environmental computation and design decisions: how can evidence be communicated clearly while preserving methods, assumptions and uncertainty?

01 / SOLAR

Solar & shading

Time-based direct solar and shading patterns, with relationships to neighboring massing and building levels.

02 / WIND

Wind conditions

Exploratory visualization of prevailing wind and airflow relationships; not a validated CFD substitute.

03 / THERMAL

UTCI comfort

Outdoor thermal-comfort interpretation, including sun-exposed versus shaded conditions and documented inputs.

03 / Research workflow

From context
to evidence-led decisions.

RSA organizes environmental information into a traceable workflow, while separating quantitative computation from explanatory interpretation.

01Site and massing

Set up site geometry, adjacent buildings and design assumptions with source information.

02Environmental analysis

Investigate solar/shading, wind-related context and thermal comfort at selected spatial and temporal scales.

03Evidence translation

Present readable spatial views and data summaries alongside uncertainty and validation status.

04Decision research

Study whether clearer environmental information improves user interpretation and early-stage design choices.

05 / Future integration

Claude as an explanation layer.
Not a simulation engine.

A planned Claude API integration would help people read environmental evidence without replacing the underlying numerical analysis, source records or professional judgment.

Planned capability / 01

Evidence-grounded interpretation

Convert structured model results, configuration settings and limitations into plain-language explanations. Distinguish computed values from AI-generated interpretation.

Planned capability / 02

Comparison & traceable reports

Summarize floor-level and scenario differences, flag data gaps, draft reviewable environmental reports and preserve references back to inputs and outputs.

Status: proposed, not yet deployed. Claude will not calculate validated solar, wind or UTCI results in place of specialist engines. Human review and explicit uncertainty reporting remain essential.

RSA / Questions

Clarity is part
of the product.

Is RSA available as a commercial software subscription?

No. RSA is presently an independently developed research prototype. RSA Core, RSA Studio and RSA Intelligence are product-architecture concepts, not subscriptions available for purchase.

Are the software screenshots real?

Yes. The prototype gallery uses screenshots from the RSA development environment. Screenshots do not by themselves establish scientific accuracy, robustness or production readiness.

Does RSA replace Radiance, CFD or energy simulation tools?

No. RSA is designed as an early-stage evidence and communication aid. Specialist simulation remains necessary for engineering-grade analysis, and the wind module is explicitly exploratory.

Is Claude API already integrated?

Not yet. Claude integration is a planned research direction to investigate how data-grounded language explanations may improve interpretation, comparison and report drafting.

Is RSA a registered company or an official university service?

No. RSA is an independent project developed by Yu-Jyun Chiu in connection with graduate architectural studies at the National University of Kaohsiung. It is not currently an incorporated company and does not claim institutional endorsement.

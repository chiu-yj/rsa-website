Corner Library

角落圖書館

A place to pause, read and belong.

01Behind the work

Design approach

People settle into corners: semi-enclosed, moderately scaled spots feel safer than the centre of a room. Seating towers and a timber truss merge structure, shelving and seating into one walkable reading system, so each reader can choose the height and distance that suits them.

Connection to research

Small changes in light, enclosure and spatial scale can transform the way people experience a place.

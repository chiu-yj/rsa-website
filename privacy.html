What this site does

research website.

Does this website use analytics or tracking cookies?

The published RSA website code does not currently include an analytics tracker, advertising pixel or a feature that intentionally sets tracking cookies. External services and your browser may handle technical information under their own policies.

What happens if I email RSA?

Clicking a contact link opens your email client. Your message, address and information you choose to provide will reach the RSA research inbox hosted by Zoho Mail, and may be used to understand and respond to your inquiry. Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive information. The website itself does not submit inquiry forms to a custom backend.

Who hosts this website?

Public website content is served through GitHub Pages, using the rsa-analyzer.com domain. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical connection information subject to their own privacy practices.

Are screenshots and links external?

RSA screenshots are site assets. The website also links to the GitHub repository and email services. When you choose to open external destinations, their applicable policies govern those services.

Can this notice change?

Yes. If the project introduces analytics, online forms, accounts, API services or commercial products, this notice should be reviewed and updated before deployment.

Research / Evidence, not assumptions

Scientific rigor is
a design decision.

RSA investigates how technical environmental data becomes understandable, traceable and useful without sacrificing scientific accountability.

01 / Research framework

Measure. Interpret.
Evaluate understanding.

The platform is positioned as an early-stage decision-support and evidence-translation tool. It does not replace specialist environmental simulation.

01 / INPUT

Document context

Define geometry, datasets, model boundaries and input assumptions.

02 / COMPUTE

Generate evidence

Run targeted analyses with methods appropriate to their stated limits.

03 / VERIFY

Test results

Compare selected outputs with reference tools and record error measures.

04 / STUDY

Study decisions

Investigate understanding through planned user evaluation.

03 / Technical environment

Connected by design.
Transparent by method.

Project research references geographic sources including OpenStreetMap and Taiwan’s NLSC, and climate references such as CWA, ERA5 and Open-Meteo where applicable. Documentation references do not establish that every API is integrated, live or independently validated.

04 / Limitations & safeguards

Good tools explain
what they cannot claim.

01 / SHADOW

Benchmark-specific

Earlier shadow comparisons do not imply verified accuracy for every date, geometry or current build.

02 / WIND

Exploratory

Wind visualization should not be treated as CFD-based pressure or pedestrian safety evidence.

03 / COMFORT

Input-dependent

Thermal comfort evaluation depends on climate inputs, radiation, geometry and modeling assumptions.

Research boundaryRSA is under active development. User studies, full-system revalidation, repeatability checks and source transparency are ongoing or planned; claims of certified engineering performance would be premature.

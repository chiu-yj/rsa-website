Encounter Treatment

相癒

Inclusive space for connection and wellbeing.

01Behind the work

Design approach

People with disabilities, carers and visitors share one curving route instead of a corridor of separate rooms. Wheelchair dimensions set the path widths, turning radii and resting points, so accessibility generates the plan rather than being added to it.

Connection to research

Comfort is both environmental and social; design should make human experience central.
